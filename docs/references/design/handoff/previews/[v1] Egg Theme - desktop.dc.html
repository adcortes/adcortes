About

Product work is rarely a straight line. It moves between people, constraints, sketches and code. I enjoy bringing those parts together until an interface feels clear to the people who use it and practical for the teams who build it.

01Who I am

A UX Engineer who started as a product designer in Rio de Janeiro and learned to build what I designed.

02What keeps me curious

Small, well-made tools that change someone's working day — and using AI to learn faster while building them.

03How I create value

I turn decisions into systems: UX architecture, tokens and components that let design and engineering move together.

04My strengths

  • Translating between stakeholders, design and engineering
  • Accessible frontend
  • Systems that grow with a team
